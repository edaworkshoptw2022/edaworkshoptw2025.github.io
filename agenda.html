Invited Talk I｜Design for AI and AI for Design
From the perspective of the semiconductor ecosystem, this presentation explores how the AI supercycle is driving an unprecedented demand for computational power, pushing the boundaries of what's possible from massive data centers to compact edge devices. As silicon designs grow to include over 300 billion transistors and adopt complex chiplet-based architectures, traditional engineering workflows are no longer sufficient to meet these demands. Cadence is at the forefront of this transformation, pioneering AI-driven solutions that are critical for engineers. By applying agentic AI and multi-agent orchestration to complex silicon and system design challenges, we are enabling the creation of the next-generation AI infrastructure and empowering the optimization of power, performance, and area for the chips that will define our future.
講師介紹｜Wilbert Chen（益華電腦 Senior Technical Marketing Manager）
Wilbert Chen is a Senior Technical Marketing Manager at Cadence Design Systems with more than 20 years of experience in IC design and advanced packaging. He previously served as Deputy R&D Director at the Electronic and Optoelectronic System Research Laboratories of Taiwan’s Industrial Technology Research Institute (ITRI). His expertise spans chip, package, and board co-design, digital IC design from specification to physical implementation, and advanced packaging for 2.5D and 3D integration. He also has experience designing high-speed data converters and SerDes IP. He has published in the IEEE Journal of Solid-State Circuits and at ISSCC, A-SSCC, and CICC, and is a named inventor on dozens of granted U.S. patents in IC design.

Invited Talk II｜我的創業之路 - From Design Service Company To AI System Company
講師介紹｜楊健盟 博士（擷發科技 董事長）
楊健盟(James Yang)博士畢業於清華大學、美國南加大，並於2006年獲全額獎學金赴紐約大學攻讀電機博士，研究晶片安全與高效能運算。創立擷發科技前，楊健盟即開發全球首顆多介面網路安全晶片，並將核心IP成功授權美國CPU設計公司，開創台灣IC業對美授權先例。創業後，他帶領團隊推出IP市集、委外設計平台與雲端協作工具，接續開發晶片架構設計工具Architecture Compiler及效能分析平台Performance Analyzer，廣獲高通、聯詠、創意等客戶肯定。近年擷發科技積極進入車載及無人機市場，推動「雙引擎策略」——CAPS（AI 跨平台部署解決方案）與CATS（客製化ASIC設計服務），並推出無程式碼AI視覺平台AIVO，降低AI應用門檻。提出「Designless」設計模式，打造可用成熟製程實現先進效能的新路徑，並完美結合Edge AI落地所需要的軟硬體整合，引領台灣邁向AI半導體設計新世代。

Invited Talk III｜Unpack Advanced Packaging: From Buzzwords to Real Challenges
Advanced packaging has become one of the hottest topics in the semiconductor industry, driven by the rapidly growing demands of AI applications. From SoC to SiP, and from chiplets to 2.5D/3D integration, numerous buzzwords are shaping the future of semiconductor technology and opening new research opportunities. But what lies behind these exciting terms? What are the real engineering challenges? In this talk, we will explore the latest technology trends, unpack the key concepts behind these buzzwords, and highlight the technical challenges and opportunities for future research. We will also introduce the resources and technology platforms available at the Taiwan Semiconductor Research Institute (TSRI) to support advanced packaging research and foster next-generation semiconductor innovation in Taiwan.
講師介紹｜劉建男 博士（台灣半導體研究中心 主任）
Dr. Chien-Nan (Jimmy) Liu is Director General of the Taiwan Semiconductor Research Institute (TSRI) and a professor in the Department of Electronics and Electrical Engineering at National Yang Ming Chiao Tung University (NYCU). His research focuses on electronic design automation, particularly modeling and synthesis of analog and mixed-signal circuits, power and noise modeling, and functional verification of system-on-chip designs. Dr. Liu received the 2011 Distinguished Young Scholar Award from the Taiwan IC Design Society and has earned multiple awards for teaching and student mentoring at National Central University and NYCU. He previously chaired both his department at NYCU and the Taiwan Chapter of ACM SIGDA.

Invited Talk IV｜AI and Agents for Multiphysics-Aware Physical Implementation
IR drop, electromigration, and thermal effects play an increasingly important role in physical implementation. Treating them with uniform voltage and temperature margins, or checking them only at signoff, over-designs most of the chip while still missing local hotspots. Closing them efficiently requires a fusion approach: fast and accurate IR, EM, and thermal estimation during placement, power-grid construction, routing, and optimization, together with IR-aware and thermal-aware timing, so that implementation engines can account for current and heat as they make decisions. This talk discusses why multiphysics closure is hard from a physical implementation perspective, and how AI and agents can help: ML-based estimators as fast surrogates inside the implementation flow, and agents for parameter tuning, flow automation, and automatic violation fixing, with signoff analysis serving as the verifier. It also examines the practical constraints of deploying them in production tools, and closes with open problems for the research community.
